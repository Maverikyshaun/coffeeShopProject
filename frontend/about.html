About

Coffee house, roastery, and neighbourhood living room.

Our story

Started with one espresso machine.

Caffè Bruno is a small-batch coffee house and roastery. Slow-poured espresso, seasonal roasts, and a room built for lingering.

We roast a few kilos at a time, every week, so nothing sits on a shelf long enough to lose its edge. The room is built the same way - unhurried, warm, and better with a second cup.
