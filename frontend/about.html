Profile

An eye for the essential.

Pearl Mccaffrey is a personal shopper and stylist with a precise, unhurried eye. Wardrobes are edited to feel effortless - quiet luxury, considered proportion, and pieces that last.

Clients come for clarity: what to keep, what to release, and what is worth adding. The result is a wardrobe that moves lightly through the day - sophisticated, very thin in silhouette, never overworked.
